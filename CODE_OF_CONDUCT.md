# Community conduct

AgentRoute contributors are expected to be respectful, specific, and
evidence-oriented. Disagreement about architecture or claims is welcome;
harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and disclosure of another
person's private data or credentials are not.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and participation that
violate these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through the
repository's security or maintainer contact channel. Reports will be handled
confidentially to the extent practical, with action proportionate to the
behavior and risk.
